# BigBasket Category Performance — Data Story

**Tableau Dashboard Link:** https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/arpan.dey2260/viz/monthlycatagoryrevenue/Sheet1#1

Across January–June 2026, Delivered-order revenue totals **INR 88,282**. Three categories meet or beat their targets: **Household Essentials** delivers INR 21,715 against INR 17,000, finishing **INR 4,715 above target (+27.74%)**; **Bakery** delivers INR 15,410 against INR 12,000, **INR 3,410 above (+28.42%)**; and **Personal Care** delivers INR 16,382 against INR 15,500, **INR 882 above (+5.69%)**. **Dairy & Eggs** is **Below Target - Watch**, at INR 14,090 versus INR 16,500, a shortfall of **INR 2,410 (-14.61%)**. **Snacks & Beverages** is **Below Target - Critical**, at INR 10,895 versus INR 13,000, a shortfall of **INR 2,105 (-16.19%)**. **Fruits & Vegetables** is also **Below Target - Critical**, at INR 9,790 versus INR 12,000, a shortfall of **INR 2,210 (-18.42%)**.

## Exactly two recommendations

1. **Protect and scale Household Essentials while testing transferable tactics.** It is the highest-revenue category at INR 21,715 and exceeds target by INR 4,715. Preserve catalog availability and promotional visibility here, then test whether its strongest merchandising tactics can be applied to weaker categories.
2. **Prioritize a recovery review for Fruits & Vegetables and Snacks & Beverages.** They are the two Critical categories, missing target by INR 2,210 and INR 2,105 respectively. Review assortment, pricing/promotion response, and supplier/product-level performance first in these two categories before adding spend broadly.
